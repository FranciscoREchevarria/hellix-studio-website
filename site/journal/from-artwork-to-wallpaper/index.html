From Generated Artwork to a Polished iPhone Wallpaper in GIMP 3

A practical look at the finishing touches that help digital artwork feel intentional, vivid and ready for a phone screen.

HellixStudio Journal · November 11, 2026

A strong digital illustration is the beginning of a wallpaper, not always the end. When an image is destined for an iPhone screen, little decisions about cropping, detail and contrast make a big difference.

Start with the composition

Before editing colors, choose your final vertical crop. Think about the clock, Dynamic Island and app icons as part of the design. Keep the important subject away from those areas when possible.

Restore contrast gently

Generated or processed illustrations can sometimes look slightly washed out. In GIMP 3, try a subtle Curves adjustment to deepen the shadows and brighten the highlights without crushing either. Check the image at phone-screen size, not only zoomed in at 100%.

Enhance without over-sharpening

Focus detail on the subject, such as architectural edges, foliage or ornament highlights. Texture in mist, snow and distant backgrounds often benefits from staying soft. A small amount of selective sharpening is usually more natural than sharpening the entire image.

Prepare the paired screen

The Lock Screen can tell the bigger story while the Home Screen uses a related scene with more negative space. Matching color temperature, saturation and the light direction helps the pair feel intentional.

Export a clean digital file

Export in the dimensions listed for your product and check the finished JPG at the actual size on an iPhone. Avoid repeated resaving, which can introduce compression artifacts. Keep a full-resolution master separately so future variants are easy to produce.
