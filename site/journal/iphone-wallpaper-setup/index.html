How to Set Matching Lock and Home Screen Wallpapers on iPhone

A simple guide to saving your wallpaper files, styling your Lock Screen, and keeping the matching Home Screen clear and beautiful.

HellixStudio Journal · November 11, 2026

A matching wallpaper pair can completely change the feeling of your phone. The key is using the Lock Screen artwork and its coordinating Home Screen image as two separate wallpapers, instead of applying the same picture to both.

1. Download the artwork

After buying a digital wallpaper on Etsy, open Etsy in a web browser on your iPhone rather than the Etsy app. Go to your purchases and download the files for your order. If you checked out as a guest, follow the download link in your Etsy receipt email.

Some HellixStudio collections include a separate download guide with additional instructions. Follow the steps provided with your particular order.

2. Save the images to Photos

Open the downloaded JPG images from the Files app, use the Share menu, and select Save Image. Repeat for the matching Home Screen artwork. Save the original files rather than screenshots to preserve quality.

3. Set your Lock Screen

Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper and select the Lock Screen image from Photos. Pinch or reposition it if necessary so the artwork works with your clock and widgets. Then add the wallpaper.

4. Set a separate Home Screen

Choose Customize Home Screen and select the matching second image. Adjust the composition, switch off Blur if you want the artwork to stay crisp, and tap Done. Menu names may differ slightly across iOS versions.

5. Enjoy the pair

A well-matched Lock and Home Screen set creates a complete little world: the Lock Screen can be richly detailed, while the Home Screen can leave space for app icons.
