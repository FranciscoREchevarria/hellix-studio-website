THE LITTLE DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before inviting a little more magic onto your screen.

Are the wallpapers physical products? +

No. These are digital downloads for your phone. No physical item is shipped.

Where do I purchase and download them? +

Every purchase happens on the HellixStudio Etsy shop. The Etsy listing shows the exact files you will receive and your download instructions.

Do the wallpapers come as matching pairs? +

Our regular sets are designed around coordinated Lock and Home Screen artwork. Bundles may contain several pairs. Always check the specific Etsy listing for the current contents.

Can I use the artwork commercially? +

Wallpaper downloads are intended for personal use only. Please do not redistribute or resell the original files.
