OUR CREATIVE STORY

Art to carry with you.

HellixStudio is an independent creative brand exploring the meeting point between watercolor texture, celestial wonder, nostalgia and magical realism.

We believe the everyday objects we spend the most time with deserve a little beauty. A quiet library, an enchanted garden, the last light in a snow-covered village — each wallpaper is imagined as a small place to return to.

We create digital iPhone wallpaper sets and collections. Our products are sold through Etsy, where you'll find the latest inclusions, downloads and support.
