Privacy policy.

How Pin Uploader, a personal tool operated by Francisco Rodríguez for HellixStudio, handles information.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pin Uploader

Pin Uploader is a personal tool run by Francisco Rodríguez on a private computer. It schedules Pinterest Pins from a Google Calendar and posts them to one Pinterest business account. It is not offered to other people.

Information the app accesses

The app accesses the Google account, Calendar events, and Drive files you connect, and the Pinterest account, boards, and Pins you connect. It can send notifications to a Discord webhook you configure.

How that information is used and shared

The app sends content to Pinterest to publish Pins and sends notifications to Discord when configured. It is not offered to other people.

How information is stored and protected

Credentials and working files are stored locally on the operator’s computer.

This website

shophellixstudio.com showcases HellixStudio artwork and provides links to digital products sold on Etsy, along with journal articles and related content. This website does not collect accounts or passwords, and it does not offer user accounts, a shopping cart, or direct payment collection.

Purchases occur on Etsy and are subject to Etsy’s own terms and privacy practices. For order-related questions, contact HellixStudio through Etsy. Links to Etsy and, when published, YouTube take you to other websites with their own privacy policies.

This website does not enable analytics unless the operator configures them. If analytics are configured, they may use cookies and send standard usage data to the analytics provider. The operator will update this policy before enabling nonessential tracking.

Contact

For questions about Pin Uploader or this privacy policy, contact Francisco Rodríguez through HellixStudio on Etsy.
