Privacy notice.

How this independent gallery website handles information.

What this website does

shophellixstudio.com showcases HellixStudio artwork and provides links to digital products sold on Etsy, along with journal articles and related content.

Contact and purchases

This site does not offer user accounts, shopping carts or direct payment collection. Purchases occur on Etsy and are subject to Etsy's own terms and privacy practices. For order-related questions, contact HellixStudio through Etsy.

Website analytics

The default downloadable site does not enable Google Analytics. If analytics are configured by the website operator, they may process standard website usage information according to the analytics provider's policies. The operator should update this notice and obtain consent where required before enabling nonessential tracking.

External links

Links to Etsy and, when published, YouTube take you to other websites with their own privacy policies.

Updates

This notice may be revised as the website evolves. For questions, please contact HellixStudio through its Etsy shop.
