Personal publishing tool

Pin Uploader

Pin Uploader is a personal tool run by Francisco Rodríguez on a private computer. It schedules Pinterest Pins from a Google Calendar and posts them to one Pinterest business account. It is not offered to other people.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The app accesses the Google account, Calendar events, and Drive files you connect, and the Pinterest account, boards, and Pins you connect. It can send notifications to a Discord webhook you configure. Credentials and working files are stored locally on the operator’s computer. The app sends content to Pinterest to publish Pins and sends notifications to Discord when configured.

This website does not collect accounts or passwords. It loads Google Tag Manager (GTM-KVP6659Q), which may use cookies and send usage data to Google.
