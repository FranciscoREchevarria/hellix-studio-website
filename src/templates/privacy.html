Pin Uploader

Pin Uploader is a personal tool run by Francisco Rodríguez on a private computer. It schedules Pinterest Pins from a Google Calendar and posts them to one Pinterest business account. It is not offered to other people.

Information the app accesses

The app accesses the Google account, Calendar events, and Drive files you connect, and the Pinterest account, boards, and Pins you connect. It can send notifications to a Discord webhook you configure.

How that information is used and shared

The app sends content to Pinterest to publish Pins and sends notifications to Discord when configured. It is not offered to other people.

How information is stored and protected

Credentials and working files are stored locally on the operator’s computer.
