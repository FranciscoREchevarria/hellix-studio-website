There's a special kind of magic in a quiet Christmas scene: warm light behind a snowy window, a bookstore dressed in greenery, or the glow of a village street at dusk. A thoughtful wallpaper can bring that little feeling of wonder to an everyday moment.

## Choose a story, not just a color

A cohesive phone aesthetic often starts with a world you want to step into. Maybe it's a snowy village, a candlelit library, or a Victorian Christmas living room. Give your Lock Screen the most dramatic composition, then choose a calmer Home Screen that feels like a continuation of the same scene.

## Look for warmth against winter light

Cranberry, antique gold, evergreen and deep blue work beautifully together. The contrast between a cool snowy evening and a glowing window makes even a small phone screen feel atmospheric.

## Make room for the clock and icons

The best artwork is designed for the phone, not just cropped to fit it. On your Lock Screen, the top of the image should give the time room to breathe. On the Home Screen, avoid putting the busiest details directly under your app icons.

## Keep the two screens connected

Matching artwork doesn't have to be identical. Repeating the same colors, lighting and a few visual details makes two different scenes feel like a pair. That's what gives a phone makeover its finished look.
